<section_guide number="2" title="Business Requirements" references="1">
<purpose>Propose the business rules, policies, and constraints the product must honor, each identified and judgeable without technology.</purpose>

<questions>
1. Ask only when a protected business decision cannot be safely proposed: which rule should the product honor?
</questions>

<workflow>
Read Section 1 and keep its target user, problem, intent, and priorities.

Work backward from the intent and propose every business requirement before asking the user to
list them. Define each as `BR-n` at the start of a table row or list item, with exact values,
units, population and conditions, Required or Optional, the observable result that shows it is
honored or violated, and its basis. Propose ordinary business candidates for approval; ask one
focused question only when several valid choices would change money, permissions, law or
regulation, privacy or safety, irreversible data meaning, an external promise, or the acceptance
boundary, or when context is missing and cannot be proposed.

When the user names a business-mandated external system, data location, or access channel, record
the business boundary it fixes and its basis. Keep a product name only when a contract, regulation,
or organization policy mandates it. Never record replaceable technology.

Do not save the Section while an acceptance-changing value or policy is unresolved; show the
question and let the user answer or defer. When no rule applies, record that in one sentence.
</workflow>

<completion required="true">Confirm every BR with its ID, required flag, criterion, and basis before saving 2.1.</completion>
</section_guide>
