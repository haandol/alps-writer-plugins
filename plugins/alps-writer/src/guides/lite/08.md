<section_guide number="8" title="Demo Scenario" references="1,2,3,4,5,6">
<purpose>Work backward from the approved requirements to propose one executable Demo Scenario that shows every Required FR.</purpose>

<questions>
1. Ask only when the demo exposes a protected decision that cannot be safely proposed: which boundary should apply?
</questions>

<workflow>
Read Sections 1-6. If Section 7 was written, read it too and keep every demonstrated claim inside
that boundary.

Reverse-engineer the shortest scenario that shows every Required FR at least once. Propose a
concrete starting state, representative demo input, sequential user actions, and visible expected
results; do not ask the user to supply them. For every step, show the user action, visible
expected result, and the FR and EC IDs it shows. Include relevant EC counterexamples when they are
needed to show that a result serves its intent. Reuse shared setup when the requirements form one
flow; use separate execution blocks when connecting them would require an unapproved business
contract.

End with one overall pass result that requires every shown FR and EC criterion to pass; Required
ECs not shown remain judged by Section 5. Present the complete scenario and its FR coverage before
approval. A passing demo shows that the approved behavior runs; never present it as proof of
business impact or market validity.

After the document is complete, mention once that a completed Lite can be the source of a derived
Full ALPS when implementation preparation is needed. This does not change Lite completion.
</workflow>
